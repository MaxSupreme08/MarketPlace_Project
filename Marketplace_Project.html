About Us

IIPS Marketplace is a student-run space to pass useful things to the next person on campus. Every listing is checked by a manager before it goes live.

  1. 1

    Post

    Add photos, price and condition. Pick a 4-digit PIN so you can remove the item later.

  2. 2

    Get approved

    A manager reviews your listing, then it appears in the live feed.

  3. 3

    Meet and sell

    Buyers call or message you on WhatsApp. Agree on a spot on campus.
